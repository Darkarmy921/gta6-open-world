Port Solano

A sun-soaked harbour city. One driver. No way out but through.

Rook is back in Port Solano with $250, a pistol, and a debt to Delia Maren. Steal cars, outrun the Solano PD, and run the six jobs that pay. Between jobs, take taxi fares and delivery gigs off the board — and mind the weather: rain makes the roads slick, and at 5 stars the PD sets roadblocks.

WASD / Arrows — move & drive  ·  E — enter / exit car, talk to Delia
Mouse — aim & shoot  ·  F — punch  ·  R — reload
Shift — run  ·  P — pause  ·  M — music
N — next radio station  ·  T — stats screen  ·  $ — side-job marker

Tip: press E to talk, enter a car, or start a job — E does all three.

An original game. Port Solano, Rook, Delia Maren and the Solano PD are invented for this game.
